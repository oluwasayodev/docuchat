Problem

Support staff spent hours on repeat questions, and answers changed from person to person.

Solution

A chat assistant that answers from approved help articles and shows which one it used.

AI component

Articles are split into chunks and embedded. The closest chunks go to an LLM API with a prompt that limits answers to that context.

My contribution

Designed the API, wrote the retrieval step, wrote the prompts and added tests.

Challenges

Stopping made-up answers. I added a rule to reply "I do not know" when no article matched.
